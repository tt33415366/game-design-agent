# Under-Sea Exploration: Gameplay Proposal

Date: 2026-10-08. Brief: [brief.md](brief.md), confirmed 2026-10-08. Classification key: sourced fact | Design Assumption | Player Validation. All experience claims are Design Assumptions; the only Player Validation in this repo's history is the Unmarked Valley U1-U5 record, which transfers only as taste evidence, not as evidence for this concept.

## Experience Candidates

| Candidate | Hook | Player action | Primary creative priority | Intentional sacrifice | Largest uncertainty | Decision status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Sunlight Shelf | Drift across a bright reef shelf, choose which sight to investigate; pressure lives in a few dark crevices and a drop-off you can skirt or brave | Free swim + investigate; arrival reveals (validated loop) | Ease and freedom; pressure as optional, visible pockets | Little structural variety — same verb everywhere | Does pressure feel meaningful if you can always avoid it? | proposed |
| B. The Tide's Rhythm | A safe reef you reshape at low tide (open channels, move stones), then a timed high-tide swim through what you built; pressure comes from the returning water | Rearrange at calm, then traverse under time pressure | Player-authored space + earned tension | Freedom is phased, not continuous; calm phase is prep, not wandering | Does setup-then-test feel like agency or like a chore gate? | proposed |
| C. Lantern Descent | Vertical dive along a fixed shaft; you carry one light and choose how deep to go each run, surfacing before air runs out; pressure rises with depth | Depth-risk management with one resource | Tension-release rhythm (descend scared, surface relieved) | Not free roaming — one axis, repeated runs | Does repeated descent stay interesting, or is the first dive the whole game? | proposed |

These are materially different: A is spatial freedom with optional pressure pockets (reuses the validated loop), B is phased build-then-traverse, C is run-based risk on a single axis. B and C are not cosmetic variants of A.

## Recommendation

**A — but with one structural addition to answer its uncertainty**: make pressure pockets *visible from safety* (dark crevice mouths, the drop-off edge) so avoiding them is a real choice, and give each pocket a reveal worth the risk. Rationale: valley U5 validated free-roam + arrival payoff for this exact user; A tests whether the same loop holds when the theme carries natural tension contrast. B risks the calm phase feeling like homework (the user's grinding aversion). C contradicts "mostly ease and freedom" as the dominant feel and risks being one-note (single axis, repeat runs).

Recommended smallest experiment: the valley v2 slice, re-skinned honestly (not a theme wrapper — water changes movement): buoyant drift physics (inertia, vertical swim), three reveals on the shelf (reef arch, wreck *exterior* silhouette — no looting, anemone garden), one pressure pocket (a dark crevice with the strongest reveal inside). Push verb carries over as "push a boulder to open a current shortcut."

## Prototype Scope and Checkpoint (pending; requires user selection + confirmation)

- Validation question (F6): initial attraction — does the user investigate unprompted, and does the crevice *tempt*; sustained interest — after the first reveal, does the user seek another sight or dare the crevice?
- Minimum playable content: one reef shelf map, buoyant swim, three reveals, one pressure pocket, one current shortcut, restart.
- Maximum iterations: propose 1. Time limit: propose 2 hours. Spending: zero. (Conservative proposals pending confirmation; the user supplied no budget this time.)
- Excluded: oxygen meter, enemies, salvage/looting, upgrades, checklist UI, touch controls.
- Checkpoint: `pending` — user selects candidate (A/B/C) and confirms scope+budget before system-design or any implementation.
