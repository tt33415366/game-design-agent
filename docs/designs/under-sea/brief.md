## Design Assumptions (labeled, revisable)

1. The validated valley loop (free roaming + arrival payoff) is a candidate starting point, not a mandate. Whether "ease and freedom with pockets of pressure" is best served by the same loop or a new structure is an open question for gameplay-design.
2. "Under sea" excludes salvage-adjacent framing (no wreck-looting loop). The pressure scenes should come from environment/threat (depth, current, darkness, oxygen) rather than resource grind.
3. No oxygen-management simulator unless the user asks; pressure should be scene-local, not a global survival meter (this is a guess at taste — flagged).
