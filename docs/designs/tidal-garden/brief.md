## Design Assumptions (labeled, revisable)

1. The calm phase must itself be pleasant, not homework: reshaping is limited to a few meaningful moves (open/close channels by moving a small number of stones), not a build menu.
2. The action phase has a soft timer (rising tide) rather than a hard fail: slow players get a less interesting swim, not a game-over (consistent with the user's grinding/punishment aversion).
3. One tide cycle is the testable unit; multiple cycles or persistent gardens are out of scope unless the first cycle validates.
4. Theme assumption: a shoreside tidal flat rendered top-down, distinct enough from Sunlight Shelf's open reef that this is not a reskin — the verb (reshape) is new, the setting is intertidal, not deep water.
