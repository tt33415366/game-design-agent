## Recommendation and Smallest Experiment

- Recommendation: `Candidate 1, The Misdirected Parcel.` It best matches the confirmed desire for discovery and cleverness with a finite, non-grinding experience that a solo developer can keep small in a desktop browser. Its content can be authored as a single unique solution, so it does not rely on procedural generation, art purchases, or replayability.
- Smallest experiment: `One local screen containing six text clue cards, four answer buttons, and a result explanation for one unique solution. Observe an unassisted participant attempting it before any explanation, only after the scope is confirmed.` This tests whether the key deduction is naturally found rather than merely solvable after instruction.
- Approach lens: `Hypothesis-led observation (Valve adaptation): does an unassisted player receive and want the intended “aha” experience?`

## Mechanic Proposal

For the recommended candidate, the player receives a compact case file containing six short notes and four possible recipients. The notes constrain recipient, time, and location. The player can mark a note as relevant or uncertain, choose a recipient, and submit once the hypothesis is ready. Correct resolution should name the decisive relation; an incorrect resolution should preserve the case state and identify the contradiction without exposing the answer. Exact legal inputs, transition order, answer handling, and feedback are deferred to a system specification after the core gameplay/prototype scope is confirmed.
