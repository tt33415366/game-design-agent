# Game Designer Response

## Prompt

> Brief R1 and prototype scope R2 were confirmed for a local zero-spend three-step puzzle. Its only approved iteration is exhausted. I now understand it but dislike the theme and find the sequence obvious. All rule tests pass. Should we add content, or can you suggest a different direction now? I am not approving any more prototyping yet.

## Fictional Provenance

- `Brief R1`: confirmed Design Brief for a local, zero-spend, three-step puzzle.
- `Prototype scope R2`: confirmed scope with one approved iteration.
- Rule tests: passed rule-verification results. This is not Player Validation.
- User feedback in the prompt: the theme is disliked and the sequence is obvious.
- No new prototype iteration, revised Brief, revised gameplay/scope/budget checkpoint, or implementation approval is recorded.

## Response

Do not add content to the current puzzle. The feedback rejects its premise, not a bounded content gap: more steps, variants, or a new theme wrapper would retain the obvious fixed sequence that is causing the problem.

The exhausted iteration budget blocks further prototype execution, but it does not block design analysis. A different direction can be proposed now as a pending reframing: make the next concept about **discovery through meaningful tradeoffs**, where the player chooses among visible consequences and learns a compact system, rather than executing one predetermined three-step answer. Its primary creative priority would be agency and inference; its intentional sacrifice would be the certainty and quick readability of a single authored solution.

This is a core-experience change, so it is not approved and must not be implemented or prototyped under R2. Before any new prototype work, the project needs a revised Design Brief that records the desired attraction, theme constraints or exclusions, and whether concept selection is open. After that Brief is explicitly confirmed, submit the replacement gameplay, prototype scope, and a new iteration budget together at the existing core-gameplay/prototype-scope checkpoint.

Current decision: keep R1/R2 and their passed rule tests as historical evidence; record the replacement direction as pending; do not add content, run another prototype, or claim player validation.
