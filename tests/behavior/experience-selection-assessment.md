# Experience-Selection Evaluation

Date: 2026-09-30. These are independent qualitative agent exercises with constructed prompts, not an automated regression suite or real Player Validation. Outputs demonstrate observed behavior in these runs, not general creative quality or reliable behavior across every model.

## Inputs and Baseline

Raw inputs: [four independent cases](../scenarios/04-experience-selection/input.md). The baseline used unchanged instructions and is retained in [experience-selection-baseline.md](experience-selection-baseline.md).

Baseline A preserved pending approvals and supplied exclusions. B already refused expansion under an exhausted budget and distinguished claimed rule-test success from experience evidence. C proposed one inexpensive deterministic puzzle, then offered tile and theme wrappers as alternatives. The observed selection gap was not missing approval discipline; it was insufficient comparison of different experiences.

## First Forward Exercise

[Initial output](experience-selection-forward-initial.md) produced experience candidates with trade-offs, but began C with a particular clue/answer loop before comparing them. D emitted a blocked full handoff for a review-only label question. Independent review also identified ambiguous brief authorization and missing preservation of a selected concept's creative priority/sacrifice. These findings motivated a focused instruction/template correction, not changes to the prototype.
