# Design Approaches

Use this reference only to choose one experience-specific lens or a smallest experiment. These are adaptations of the source material, not claims about any studio's internal practice, employment, or guaranteed outcome. See [the research record](../../docs/research/2026-09-30-studio-design-workflows.md).

| Adapted approach | Evaluative question | Trade-off | Smallest experiment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Intention and readable exceptions (Nintendo adaptation) | What will the player naturally try, and can they read what is intentionally unavailable? | Supporting plausible actions increases implementation and verification work. | One interaction with a visible exception; observe unprompted attempts. |
| Hypothesis-led observation (Valve adaptation) | Does an unassisted player receive and want the intended experience? | Participant access and interpretation are required. | A rough short slice, observed before explanation. |
| Authored challenge and preparation (FromSoftware adaptation) | What can the player learn or change before overcoming this situation? | Routes and preparations increase balancing work. | One encounter with one learnable preparation. |
| Bounded systemic situation (Larian adaptation) | Which attempted solutions have meaningful consequences within this small situation? | Options create downstream content and testing obligations. | A whiteboxed situation with two interacting rules. |
| Emotional contrast through interaction (Naughty Dog adaptation) | What emotional change should this scene create, and does the interaction support it? | Bespoke presentation can be costly. | One playable beat testing the change without cinematic production. |
| Renewed attempts and consequence (Supergiant adaptation) | What makes another attempt desirable besides correcting an error? | Variety and continuity require content and state work. | A short repeatable cycle with one meaningful variation or consequence. |

Choose the approach that serves the selected experience; do not combine the list into a default style. A unique solution or a non-repeatable experience can be the right fit when that is the intended appeal.
